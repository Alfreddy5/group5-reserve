PParkEase

Find a spot. Pay for it. Park with no guesswork.

ParkEase shows every slot in the lot in real time. Pick one, pay the fee, and it's yours right away with a ticket code. If the lot is full, join the waiting list and you get first pick.

How it works

  1. Pick a slot. Tap a green slot and choose how many hours you need.
  2. Pay the fee. Pay with GCash or card.
  3. Slot is Occupied. It's yours the moment you pay.
  4. Keep your ticket code. It looks like PE-7K3Q9X and is saved in My Account.
